# Better Grades

Better Grades is a free academic answer, explanation, calculator, practice, and diagnostic platform. The current library spans Algebra and Calculus, with every useful answer connected to its topic, related methods, tools, and practice.

## Product areas

- One typed search index across 72 guides, topic maps, interactive tools, practice, and direct answers
- Six organized Algebra topics and six organized Calculus topics
- Four editorial article archetypes: direct answer, concept, method, and decision guide
- Full canonical solution for the integral of sec³x
- Integration-by-parts method guide
- Functional Integration Method Finder
- Browser-side Algebra Expression Checker with keyboard-math and raw LaTeX entry
- Ten-question method-selection practice
- Twelve-question calculus-readiness diagnostic
- Twenty-problem timed or untimed Integration Bee
- Auto, light, and dark themes
- Device-local practice progress; no account required

The content registry separates domains, topics, resources, tools, assessments, public routes, aliases, review state, search terms, and related-resource links. Registry validation catches duplicate paths, missing topic sequences, invalid relationships, and articles that are not represented in the public catalog.
