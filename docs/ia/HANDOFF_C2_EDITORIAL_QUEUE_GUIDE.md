# Handoff 2 editorial queue guide

Review the 234 article/lesson rows individually. Confirm role complementarity, shared concept and skills, destination completeness, placement, and anchor. Leave a decision of approve, reject, or revise with notes.

The lesson companion file is a ranked review view, not the raw evidence. It retains at most the best candidate per lesson/category: practice, quick explanation, worked example, reference, and tool/visual. The complete 2,522-row audit file remains unchanged.

Approval is never inferred from a score. A later implementation must change `editorialStatus` explicitly and rerun graph/findability validation.
