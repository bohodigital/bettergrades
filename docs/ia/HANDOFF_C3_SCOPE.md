## Decision rules

`KEEP_DISTINCT`, `NARROW`, `REWRITE`, and `DEFER_WITH_REASON` are non-destructive dispositions. Any `MERGE`, `REDIRECT`, `CONSOLIDATE`, or `DELETE` decision requires the exact affected route set, rationale, redirect map, and owner approval before implementation.

The initial implementation target is zero destructive decisions. Shared inferred concepts are not sufficient evidence that routes have the same learner intent.
