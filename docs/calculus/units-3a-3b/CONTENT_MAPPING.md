## Unit 3A textbook sequence

The imported core sequence covers:

- orientation and the integral roadmap;
- antiderivatives, initial values, total change, displacement, and distance;
- partitions, Riemann sums, definite integrals, signed area, properties, and average value;
- accumulation functions and both parts of the Fundamental Theorem;
- basic rules, substitution, integration by parts, trigonometric methods, partial fractions, and strategy selection;
- midpoint, trapezoidal, and Simpson approximation; and
- improper integrals over infinite intervals and with unbounded integrands.

Review, cumulative practice, two practice exams, two public answer keys, and focused in-depth articles follow the core textbook map. Exercise-only pages receive attempt-gated answer reveals. Exposition and section-specific reading lenses remain visible before problem sets.

Generated learner pages are semantic typed nodes. Raw LaTeX, TikZ, PGFPlots, source filenames, and unsupported environments fail import or release checks rather than appearing in the browser.
