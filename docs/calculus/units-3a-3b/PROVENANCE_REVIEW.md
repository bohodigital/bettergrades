# Provenance review

The installed `content/calculus/units/unit-3a/handoff/` files are an immutable historical intake snapshot. Their `draft` release fields, empty route visual arrays, and clipped source descriptions are not production state. The generated public registry, compiled pages, derived visual placement, and release reports are the authoritative runtime artifacts.

The normalized handoff's `provenance.json`, `SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`, and `migration/RIGHTS_REVIEW.md` were preserved in the controlled intake area and reviewed before import.

Public route artifacts retain only safe composition status. Source paths, archive contents, editorial PDFs, source textbook PDFs, and private solution/rubric payloads are excluded from public assets, browser bundles, and Pages output.

The importer treats every learner page, assessment, solution, answer key, and visual as a typed record with an explicit provenance state. Existing Better Grades focused articles remain original registered content at their prior canonical routes. The release does not claim public-domain status or silently flatten adapted and original material into one undifferentiated corpus.
