# Article and lesson intent map

## Decision rule

Older pages are preserved by default when search and inbound-link evidence is unavailable. A redirect is retained only where the repository already contains a reviewed Unit 4 consolidation decision and the source has already been removed from the public registry. Retained pages remain self-canonical and must serve a narrower intent than the course lesson.

## Decisions

Five previously approved Unit 4 consolidations remain one-hop `308` redirects:

- the old Chapter 4 hub to the Unit 4A map;
- the old geometric-series guide to the complete Unit 4A lesson;
- the old convergence-test chooser to the complete Unit 4A decision lesson;
- the old power-series interval guide to the complete Unit 4B lesson;
- the old Taylor remainder guide to the complete Unit 4B theorem lesson.

Two older concise articles remain distinct:

- `harmonic-series-diverges` is a focused proof supporting the broader harmonic and p-series lesson;
- `ratio-test-vs-root-test` is a focused comparison supporting the broader test-selection lesson.

Each retained route is self-canonical and connected to the course. Their narrower proof/comparison framing is materially different from the full lesson role. No additional redirect was introduced without Search Console or inbound-link evidence.

The complete required field set and evidence status are in `data/seo/article-lesson-intent-map.csv`. Similarity is explicitly marked unavailable where a redirect source had already been removed, rather than fabricated.
