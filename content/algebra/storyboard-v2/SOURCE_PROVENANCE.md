## Mathematical/editorial source corpus

The course was planned from the saved 19-PDF Algebra/Precalculus corpus, especially ORCCA Parts II-III, Katherine Yoshiwara's Elementary Algebra and Modeling, Functions, and Graphs, Ivan Zaigralin's Basic Algebra with Applications, Burzynski and Ellis's Fundamentals of Mathematics, John Redden's Advanced Algebra, Stitz-Zeager's prerequisite and precalculus material, and the Lippman-Rasmussen function-centered precalculus sequence.

Licensing and attribution must be handled at the exact source-fragment level. ORCCA-specific accessible-math attribution requirements, GFDL obligations, share-alike terms, noncommercial restrictions, and reference-only sources remain binding. The implementation must not copy a source merely because it was free to download.

## Provenance rule

Every adapted passage, exercise, and visual must carry a source record or an explicit original-work declaration. Public attribution must be generated where the source license requires it. Noncommercial and all-rights-reserved sources may guide independent editorial planning but may not supply derivative production copy without permission.
