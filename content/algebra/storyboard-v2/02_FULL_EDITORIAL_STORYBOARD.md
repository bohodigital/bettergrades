# A0 - Arithmetic Readiness and Repair

**Narrative act:** Prologue - Making quantity dependable  
**Role:** Adaptive support layer with complete instruction for students who need numerical repair.  
**Governing question:** What must be dependable about numbers before symbols can carry meaning?

## Unit story

The learner stabilizes numerical meaning: signed quantities, multiplicative structure, fractions, rates, units, powers, and roots. Diagnostic routing allows a student to enter only the pages needed and then return to the main Algebra spine.

## Unit outcomes

- Interpret signed numbers as position, direction, and change.
- Compute with whole numbers, fractions, decimals, and percentages while estimating plausibility.
- Use factors, multiples, ratios, rates, and units structurally.
- Interpret powers and roots and use scientific notation.
- Recognize when arithmetic output is unreasonable or dimensionally impossible.

## Source basis

- Burzynski and Ellis, Fundamentals of Mathematics: complete arithmetic instruction, proficiency exams, and solution-rich practice.
- Yoshiwara, Elementary Algebra: just-in-time arithmetic warm-ups tied to later algebraic lessons.
- Zaigralin, Basic Algebra with Applications: concise conceptual bridge from arithmetic to algebra.

## Algebra-Precalculus boundary

Algebra ends with reliable manipulation, solving, introductory graph interpretation, function notation, domains, exponential models, and logarithm laws. Precalculus begins with functions as objects and develops transformations, composition, inverses, global behavior, polynomial zeros and multiplicity, rational asymptotics, comparative growth, trigonometric functions, vectors, polar/parametric structure, and direct calculus readiness.
